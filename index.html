Good to know

Getting there

By car

Park at the Resort Carpark. Parking is paid: S$5 for the first hour (includes a 20-minute grace period), then S$2.50 for every hour after that.

By Grab or taxi

Ask to be dropped off at Mandai Rainforest Resort. It has its own drop-off and pick-up point.

By MRT and shuttle

Take the MRT to Khatib (NS14), then hop on the Mandai Khatib Bus (M2) at Exit A (S$2.50). Buses that go straight to the resort run hourly, and the last one leaves Khatib at 6:30pm, so plan to arrive early. Public buses 138 (from Ang Mo Kio or Springleaf MRT) and 927 (from Choa Chu Kang MRT) stop at the Mandai Transport Hub.

Going home

The evening ends at 10:45pm. The last M2 bus back to Khatib MRT leaves Mandai Wildlife EAST, a short walk from the resort, at 12:00am. Grab and taxis can pick you up at the resort.

Bus timings are from Mandai's website and may change. We'll re-check them closer to the day.
